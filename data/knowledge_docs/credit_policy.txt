CREDIT POLICY — FIELD SALES REFERENCE

=== CREDIT LIMITS AND TERMS ===

Standard credit terms: Net 30 days
Extended terms available for: Tier-1 wholesalers only, requires manager approval

Credit limit tiers:
  Small (Minimarket/Corner store): INR 4,00,000 – INR 8,00,000
  Medium (Retailer/Supermarket):   INR 8,00,000 – INR 16,00,000
  Large (Wholesaler/Key account):  INR 16,00,000 – INR 40,00,000

=== ORDER RULES BY CREDIT UTILIZATION ===

Utilization below 70%:
  - Full order allowed
  - No approval needed
  - Standard process

Utilization 70% to 85%:
  - Order allowed but flag to supervisor
  - Remind customer of payment due date before confirming order

Utilization 85% to 95%:
  - Order can proceed ONLY if customer pays outstanding balance on visit
  - Collect at least 50% of outstanding before releasing order
  - Document payment on visit record

Utilization above 95% — CREDIT BLOCK:
  - Do NOT place order
  - Escalate to credit controller before leaving outlet
  - Offer cash-only order as alternative
  - Do not accept post-dated cheques

=== CHURN AND RECOVERY ACTIONS ===

Customer not ordered in 15 to 30 days:
  - Check for competitor activity at outlet
  - Verify stock levels — stockpile may still be present
  - Soft follow-up: What can we do to support your order this week?

Customer not ordered in 31 to 45 days:
  - Flag as at-risk in visit report
  - Bring product samples or promotional offer
  - Escalate to Area Sales Manager if two consecutive visits with no order

Customer not ordered in 45 or more days:
  - Formal churn risk — notify ASM immediately
  - Structured recovery visit required
  - Consider credit limit review if outstanding balance is high

=== DISCOUNT AUTHORITY ===

Field rep:           Up to 5% discount, no approval needed
Area Sales Manager:  Up to 10% discount
Regional Manager:    Up to 15% discount
Country Manager:     Above 15%, exceptional cases only

FOC (Free of Charge):
  - Up to 2% of invoice value — field rep authority
  - Must be recorded as INV_FOC_AMT on invoice

=== RETURNS POLICY ===

Damaged goods on delivery:
  - Accept return immediately, issue credit note on same visit
  - Photograph damage before accepting

Short-dated stock less than 3 months to expiry:
  - Field rep may accept return with supervisor approval
  - No return accepted on frozen or chilled product once cold chain broken

Customer changed mind:
  - No return accepted once goods delivered and signed
