SALES GUIDELINES — FIELD SALES REFERENCE

=== WHAT IS THIS FILE? ===

This file contains the day-to-day selling strategies, visit objectives,
upsell guidance, and KPI targets for field sales representatives.
The agent uses this to answer questions like:
- What should I recommend for this customer type?
- How much should I suggest ordering?
- What are my targets this month?

=== PRE-VISIT PREPARATION ===

Before every visit:
1. Review customer's last 3 orders — look for dropped SKUs
2. Check outstanding balance — do not arrive uninformed about credit
3. Review next visit date vs actual visit — are you on schedule?
4. Note seasonal period — Q4 October to December requires early stock building
5. Check inventory at distributor for fast-moving SKUs before promising stock

=== VISIT OBJECTIVES ===

Every visit should achieve at least ONE of:
  A. Order placed — primary objective
  B. Payment collected on overdue balance
  C. Distribution gained — new SKU placed at outlet
  D. Return resolved with credit note
  E. Relationship maintenance if customer is already well stocked

=== ORDER QUANTITY RECOMMENDATION ===

Baseline: use the customer's average monthly sales (AMS) as anchor
  - AMS is available in system under M_MTHCUST table
  - Suggest order = AMS multiplied by days until next visit divided by 30
  - Add 20% buffer if Q4 or festive season is upcoming

Seasonal uplift guidelines:
  - November: add 20% on beverages and confectionery
  - December: add 30% on all categories
  - Diwali season October to November: add 25% on confectionery and beverages
  - Ramadan market dependent: add 15% on dairy and culinary

=== UPSELL AND CROSS-SELL PRIORITY ===

By customer type:

  Minimarket:
    Core SKUs: PRD-002, PRD-007, PRD-009, PRD-015
    Upsell to: PRD-001 if volume warrants 2kg size
    Cross-sell: PRD-012 with PRD-009 for confectionery bundle

  Supermarket:
    Core SKUs: PRD-001, PRD-006, PRD-008, PRD-011, PRD-017
    Upsell to: PRD-019 premium coffee for high margin
    Cross-sell: PRD-018 oat drink with PRD-017 cereal as health bundle

  Wholesaler:
    Core SKUs: PRD-001, PRD-005, PRD-007, PRD-013
    Upsell to: bulk packs and extended credit terms for large orders
    Cross-sell: category bundles combining beverages, dairy, and culinary

  Retailer:
    Core SKUs: PRD-002, PRD-007, PRD-009, PRD-012, PRD-016
    Upsell to: PRD-003 instant drink mix for higher margin
    Cross-sell: PRD-013 with PRD-016 for cooking combo bundle

  HoReCa and Food service:
    Core SKUs: PRD-005, PRD-013, PRD-014, PRD-020
    Relationship is key — visit frequency matters more than per-visit order size

=== CHURN PREVENTION ===

Red flags to watch for on visit:
  - Competitor products taking shelf space your products had last visit
  - Customer mentions cash flow problems — credit review needed
  - SKU was ordered 3 months ago but not since — potential dropout
  - Customer not present on multiple consecutive visits — investigate

Recovery talking points:
  - Our data shows you ordered X units of Y in Q4 last year.
    Would you like to pre-position stock now?
  - We have a limited promotional offer this week.
    5% discount if you order before Friday.
  - Your average monthly sales is X.
    You are tracking below your average this month. How can I help?

=== KPI TARGETS ===

  Strike rate:       More than 80% of visits result in an order
  Distribution:      All core SKUs present at every outlet
  Credit collection: Outstanding below 60% of credit limit
  Churn rate:        Less than 5% of active customers going 45 or more days without order

=== REPORTING ===

Same-day reporting required after every visit:
  - Order placed or reason if not placed
  - Payment collected
  - Competitor observations
  - Outlet condition including shelf space and display quality
